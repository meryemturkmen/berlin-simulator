# Data sources and licenses

Everything in `public/assets/` is generated by `pnpm assets:build` from the open data below. No third-party models, textures or audio files are shipped.

## Buildings

- **3D-Gebäudemodelle im Level of Detail 2 (LoD 2)**, Geoportal Berlin, Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen Berlin.
- Download service: <https://gdi.berlin.de/data/a_lod2/atom/>
- License: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – Version 2.0 (<https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0>). No attribution is required; we credit the source anyway.
- Used in: `places/*.glb` (building geometry, simplified and restyled).

## Ground and roof imagery

- **Digitale farbige TrueOrthophotos 2024 (DOP20RGBI)**, Geoportal Berlin, Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen Berlin.
- Service: <https://gdi.berlin.de/services/wms/truedop_2024>
- License: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – Version 2.0.
- Used in: `places/*-ground.jpg` (aerial photo of each place at 20 cm per pixel, shown on the ground and on roofs).

## District boundaries

- **ALKIS Berlin Bezirke** (WFS), Geoportal Berlin.
- Service: <https://gdi.berlin.de/services/wfs/alkis_bezirke>
- License: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – Version 2.0.
- Used in: `map/berlin.json`.

## Streets, paths, green, water, trees, benches, lamps

- **OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
- License: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.
- Used in: `places/*.glb` (ground layers) and `places/*.json` (tree, bench, lamp and walkway positions). These files are a produced work and derivative database of OpenStreetMap data and are available under the ODbL.

## Characters, props, sound

- Avatars, NPCs, the dog, furniture, shop fronts and trees are procedural geometry defined in this repository's source code.
- All sound is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- No CC0 packs are used. (A CC0 character pack was the first choice, but no source allowed scripted downloads; see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, D4.)

## Fonts

- **Fraunces** and **DM Sans**, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted at build time through `next/font`.

## Fiction

All people, cafés and shops in the game are invented. Any resemblance to real persons or businesses is coincidental.
